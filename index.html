Current Role

Research Scientist

I am a Research Scientist at the Center for Space Physics, Boston University. My work primarily uses in-situ spacecraft data, especially NASA's MAVEN measurements, to analyze the characteristics of the Martian space environment, solar-wind-driven plasma dynamics at Mars, and ion acceleration and escape processes.

Research

Questions I Work On

My research focuses on planetary space physics, especially electromagnetic environments, solar wind coupling, plasma dynamics, and atmospheric escape.

01

Planetary Space Environments

Characterizing electromagnetic fields, plasma environments, and their dynamics around planets.

02

Solar Wind-Planet Interaction

Investigating mass and energy transfer and conversion between the solar wind and planetary systems.

03

Atmospheric Escape

Studying how solar energy input, including solar wind forcing and EUV radiation, drives atmospheric escape and shapes long-term planetary atmospheric evolution.
